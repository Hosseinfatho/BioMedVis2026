StateSignature provides two complementary views for each Markov state. The Residues tab uses precomputed Cα–Cα distance histograms (state_signature_residuals.json) to score every residue pair by its stable fraction—the share of in-state frames whose distance stays within a user-adjustable tolerance (default 1%) of the pair mean μ. Results appear as a 42×42 heatmap (red = more stable) and a top-10 list, highlighting structural signatures of the state. The Frames tab shows, per trajectory, how many frames belong to that state (from runtime MSM labels) as stacked bars split by preprocess (state_trajectory_frame_counts.json) into green (closest ~10% to the state’s structural centroid) and blue (the rest), revealing which trajectories populate the state and which frames are structurally typical vs. outliers. Clicking a cell or bar links to a representative frame in Viewer3D.

**Tolerance & 3D linking:** On Residues, the stability tolerance slider (0.1–50%, default 1%) re-scores the matrix at runtime without re-running preprocess; clicking any pair cell opens the state’s representative structure in Viewer3D and highlights both Cα positions with a dashed cylinder.
